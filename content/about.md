I’m pretty sure my first word as a baby was “Why”. I don’t remember that age too well, but it would make a lot of sense. I grew up in Canberra, surrounded by politicians, which leaves you with a lot of questions, and I’m still asking why.

Asking why is fun, but what really scratches that special part of my brain is understanding why. Strategy is the one job where that’s the whole point.

I look at a boring stat and hunt for the human truth hiding in it, then turn it into an insight a campaign can be built on. And just because an insight is good enough doesn’t mean it can’t be better, so I keep digging until it is. You can see how in **Entry Level Expert** and **Tattoos Don’t Age Well**.

I am as ambitious as they come. I wanted to be in the creative industry, so I moved to Melbourne to make it happen. I wanted to learn what communications looks like in the real workforce, so I got an internship almost two hours away, and I loved every moment.

My goal is a career where I never stop understanding new whys. Strategy at a creative agency is that career, because every new client is a new audience, a new framing, a new reason to ask why, and more importantly, to understand why.
