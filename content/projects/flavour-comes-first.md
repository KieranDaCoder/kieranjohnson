## Problem

Mixers are the least thought about part of a drink. People choose their spirit carefully and then grab whatever mixer is in the cupboard, so it’s hard for a mixer to stand out.

## Insight

The mixer makes up most of the glass but is treated as an afterthought. Even in the name G&T, the gin comes first, so the mixer is always the supporting act.

## Idea

Flip the convention and put the mixer first. Call it a T&G.

## Solution

A poster that breaks the naming convention of the G&T, one of Australia’s most popular drinks, with the headline “Call it a T&G” above a single oversized Tonic No.8 bottle. The line “Tastes so good, it comes first” lets people connect the flip back to the flavour.
