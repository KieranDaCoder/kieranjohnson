A personal project in response to a Monotype brief.

## Challenge

People love to read, but the typography behind it goes unnoticed. Monotype’s research found 69% of Gen Z and 72% of Millennials say fonts help words say what they mean. How do you make people value typography?

## Insight

Good typography is invisible until it’s gone. Spacing is the clearest case, because every reader relies on it and almost no one notices it.

## Idea

Make the value of typography unavoidable. Take it away so people feel the difficulty, then show what was missing.

## Execution

Two panels, each written as an appreciation of typography but set in packed, unspaced type, so it’s close to impossible to read. Most people skip to the highlighted line, get the point, then go back and try to read it properly. That second attempt is where they start to recognise the value. The billboard makes the case for typography and lands on you need good typography. The metrolite zooms in on the space itself, as an ode to the gap between words.
