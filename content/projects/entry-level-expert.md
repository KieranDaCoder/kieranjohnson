## Challenge

SEEK wanted to be a career partner to Gen Z jobseekers, but the main platform is too broad to speak to them. We narrowed to SEEK Grad and to ambitious graduates.

## Insight

Entry level roles in jobs involving AI are 7x more likely to ask for later-career skills. Entry level listings routinely ask for 3 or more years of experience. And there are fewer roles to go around.

## Idea

With fewer roles and growing requirements, it’s as if entry level jobs are really hiring experts. So we turned that into a badge of pride. Ambitious graduates who build the skills become entry level experts, and SEEK Grad is where they do it.

## Solution

Entry Level Expert celebrates graduates the way they deserve, with flowers, confetti and a name for what they’ve become. It runs as posters like the one above, as social posts, and as one unbroken 30 second shot through offices in different industries, each with an entry level expert being celebrated.
